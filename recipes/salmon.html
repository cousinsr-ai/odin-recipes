Salmon

Description

This is salmon on a plate and usually I make it with brocolli (or my wife makes it), with brocolli and lovely sweet potatoe fries (and a drizzle of mayo)

Ingredients

  • Salmon
  • Sweet Potatoe
  • Brocolli
  1. Cook the Sweet Potatoes in microwave
  2. Put the salmon in the air fryer at 180/200 C for 12 mins
  3. Boil the brocolli for approximately 5 minutes
  4. Serve
